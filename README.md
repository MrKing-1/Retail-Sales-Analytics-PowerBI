# Retail Sales Analytics Dashboard

## Project Overview

This project is a Power BI Retail Sales Analytics Dashboard designed to analyze sales performance, customer demographics, product category contribution, and revenue trends.

The dashboard transforms raw retail data into actionable business insights using Power Query for data preparation and Power BI for data modeling, visualization, and interactive analysis.

---

# Objectives

- Analyze overall retail sales performance.
- Identify top-performing product categories.
- Evaluate customer purchasing patterns.
- Analyze revenue trends across months and years.
- Understand customer distribution by age group and gender.
- Generate recommendations to improve business performance.

# Key Insights

1. Electronics generated the highest sales value ($156.91K) despite lower quantity compared to Clothing, indicating stronger revenue per unit.

2. Clothing achieved similar revenue performance with higher sales volume, suggesting lower value per unit.

3. Mature Adults represented the largest customer segment, showing stronger engagement compared to younger groups.

4. Revenue peaked in May ($53.15K) but declined in March, indicating monthly demand fluctuations.

---

# Recommendations

1. Prioritize Electronics upselling strategies due to higher unit value.

2. Create Clothing bundle offers to increase revenue per customer.

3. Maintain engagement with Mature Adult customers while testing targeted campaigns for younger segments.

4. Analyze successful periods like May and replicate effective strategies during weaker months.
